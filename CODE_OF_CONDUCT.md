# Community conduct

Discuss technical evidence respectfully. Critique code and behavior rather than
people. Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, and disclosure of another
person's private information are not welcome.

Give others room to correct mistakes. Keep issues and reviews relevant to the
project. Do not post private logs or identifiers without permission.

Contact the maintainer listed in README.md privately about conduct concerns.
Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close discussions, or
restrict participation. Decisions should be proportionate and explained where
doing so does not expose private information.
